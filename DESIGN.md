# ReGenCity visual system

ReGenCity uses a calm engineering register: warm near-white canvas, deep ink, and blue energy accents. Plus Jakarta Sans is the only UI typeface. The signature ribbon connects the three node cards and selected equipment states.

Runtime token mapping lives in `src/styles.css` (`--canvas`, `--surface`, `--ink`, `--primary`, `--accent`, `--mist`, `--line`, `--success`, `--warning`, `--danger`). Cards use 18–24px radii and controls use 10–12px radii. Motion is short and optional; all camera motion respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.
